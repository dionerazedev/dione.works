# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Prospective clients, employers, and collaborators evaluating Dione Raze Oro for full-stack development, AI automation, product work, and remote opportunities. They need to understand what Dione builds, inspect credible evidence, and reach him quickly.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio brings Dione's verified product work, automation prototypes, technical capabilities, experience, credentials, and contact paths into one responsive website. Success means a visitor can quickly identify Dione's focus, assess real work through project previews and case studies, and take a clear next step through email, scheduling, GitHub, LinkedIn, or résumé download.

## Positioning

Dione works across the complete path from interface and product structure to data, integrations, AI-assisted workflows, deployment, and operational handoff. The portfolio demonstrates that range through live products, source links, documented prototypes, and status labels rather than unsupported claims.

## Operating Context

The site is evaluated on desktop and mobile, shared through direct project URLs, scanned by hiring and client audiences, and explored through section navigation, case studies, a command palette, and a portfolio assistant.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS 4, Framer Motion, React Router, and Phosphor Icons.
- Preserve homepage hash destinations and every existing `/work/:slug` route.
- Preserve live-demo, source, walkthrough, presentation, résumé, scheduling, email, GitHub, LinkedIn, and secondary profile links.
- Preserve the portfolio assistant, command palette, mobile menu, light/dark themes, SEO metadata, Open Graph metadata, robots, and sitemap.
- Use only repository-verified content. Do not invent employers, clients, metrics, dates, achievements, testimonials, project outcomes, contribution totals, or unavailable projects.
- OpsPilot AI, FlowMetrics, Customer Intelligence Dashboard, Shopify development, and a self-taught biography are not currently supported by repository evidence.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Dione Raze Oro; shortened wordmark may use “Dione Raze.”
- Professional focus: AI Automation & Full-Stack Developer / Engineer.
- Based in the Philippines; repository metadata and contact copy identify Davao City.
- Binding reference: Bryl Lim's portfolio for minimalist editorial structure, page rhythm, compact navigation, dividers, information density, and restrained interaction—but never its identity, copy, projects, data, images, or code.
- The visual system is strictly monochrome. Emphasis comes from inversion, typography, arrows, rules, and halftone texture rather than accent color.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence before assertion.
2. Make the work understandable within the first viewport and inspectable through case studies.
3. Keep every interaction fast, keyboard-accessible, and usable on mobile.
4. Treat status labels and disclosures as part of the product's credibility.
5. Preserve direct contact and résumé access throughout the experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic HTML, correct heading hierarchy, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, focus-trapped dialogs, descriptive image alternatives, sufficient contrast, 44px minimum mobile targets, reduced-motion support, and no information available only on hover are required.
